Delete your data

WordLoop: Daily Vocabulary · published by Burak Can Köksaldı

In short: WordLoop has no accounts. Almost everything lives on your device and is removed when you delete the app. The only data held off-device is your anonymous purchase record at RevenueCat, which we delete on request.

Data on your device

Your language pair, level, saved words, progress, streak and preferences are stored only on your device.

  • To delete individual words, remove them in the app (My Words).
  • To delete everything, uninstall WordLoop. This permanently removes all on-device data.

Purchase record (RevenueCat)

If you started a subscription or bought lifetime access, RevenueCat keeps a record linked to a random, anonymous app user ID so your purchase can be verified and restored. To request deletion:

  1. Email burakkoksaldi@gmail.com with the subject “WordLoop data deletion”.
  2. Include the Google Play order number (GPA.…) or App Store receipt from your purchase confirmation email, so we can find the record. You do not need to send any other personal information.
  3. We delete the RevenueCat customer record within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is kept

Google Play and the Apple App Store keep their own transaction records for tax, accounting and fraud-prevention purposes under their own policies; we cannot delete those. Deleting the RevenueCat record means Pro can no longer be restored automatically — contact us again if you later need access restored.
