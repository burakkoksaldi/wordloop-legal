WORDLOOP

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

In short: WordLoop has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. Your saved words, progress and preferences stay on your device. We only use two service providers: Supabase to deliver vocabulary content, and RevenueCat to manage in-app purchases.

Who we are

WordLoop (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is published by Burak Köksaldı. For any privacy question or request, contact us at burakkoksaldi@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following on your device only. We cannot access it and it is not uploaded to us:

  • the language you speak and the language you are learning;
  • your level-check answers, detected level and daily word goal;
  • words you like, want to learn or need to review, and your daily progress and streak;
  • reminder, widget and app-guide preferences.

To show today’s word on your Home Screen widget, the app shares the current word and its translation with the widget on the same device (on iOS through a private App Group container). Deleting the app removes all of this data.

Vocabulary content (Supabase)

Words, translations and example sentences are loaded from our database hosted by Supabase. These requests contain only what is needed to return content, such as the language pair and word identifiers. As with any internet request, Supabase processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver and secure the service. We do not send your saved words, progress or any profile information.

Purchases (Apple, Google and RevenueCat)

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details. We use RevenueCat to verify purchases and determine whether WordLoop Pro is active. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID, purchase and transaction details from the store, and basic device and app information (such as platform, app version, country and currency) and processes IP addresses to provide its service.

Notifications

Learning reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We do not use push notification servers or collect notification tokens. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your device settings.

Pronunciation

Pronunciation uses your device’s built-in text-to-speech engine. The word being spoken is handled by your operating system according to its own privacy terms.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or collect your name, email address or contacts.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data.
  • We do not request location, camera, microphone or photo access.

Legal basis, retention and transfers

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR or Türkiye’s KVKK apply, we process the limited data above to provide the app and its purchases (performance of a contract) and to keep the service secure (legitimate interests). On-device data stays until you delete it or the app. Purchase records are kept by RevenueCat and the stores for as long as needed to provide your purchase and meet legal obligations. Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States and the European Union, under appropriate safeguards.

Your choices and rights

You can delete on-device data at any time by removing words in the app or deleting the app. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal data. Because we do not hold an account for you, most requests concern purchase records: contact burakkoksaldi@gmail.com and we will help, including with deletion of your RevenueCat purchase record. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

WordLoop is a general-audience learning app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We will update the effective date above and, for material changes, let you know in the app.
